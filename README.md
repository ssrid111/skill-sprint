# RoleReady

A career learning coach for Cubbie in iMessage. Choose a company and role; get a sourced skill roadmap, learning resources in your preferred format, three ranked project options, and one manageable next step. Send voice notes for goals, blockers, and explain-backs.

## What makes it different

Connects actual role requirements to learning and inspectable project evidence. It distinguishes required from preferred skills, verifies resource links, and keeps long plans in a document instead of flooding the conversation. Project scores are an explained coaching rubric, not hiring predictions.

## Install on Cubbie

Text https://github.com/ssrid111/TECH-FOMO-Filter- to Cubbie. Review Cubbie's installation prompt and reply yes. In a new message, text:

“I want to prepare for an ML internship at NVIDIA. Help me choose what to learn.”

Cubbie asks one question at a time. You can send voice notes and choose video, reading, or mixed learning. No extra key, package, backend, or connected account is required. Incoming audio is transcribed; outgoing replies are short text.

## Try the flow

1. Set a specific company, role, level, and location.
2. Share your current skills by text, resume, repository, or voice note.
3. Choose time available and learning format.
4. Review the three top gaps; request the full roadmap document if wanted.
5. Choose a ranked project and complete its first small step.
6. Send evidence or a voice explanation; receive one correction and the next step.
7. Optionally approve a check-in schedule.

Official job descriptions and learning resources are researched during use. Examples are not current employer requirements. Access limitations and stale sources must be labeled. This project makes no employment guarantees.

## Privacy and control

Progress stays in your Cubbie context, with your permission. No team server receives your resume, audio, or progress. Check-ins require consent. Sending, applying, publishing, booking, account changes, and payment require your confirmation.
