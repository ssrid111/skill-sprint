# Tech FOMO Filter

### Stop chasing every technology. Start building toward your goal.

Tech FOMO Filter is a career learning Skill for [Cubbie](https://cubbie.ai), delivered through iMessage. Tell it the company and role you're aiming for. It helps you focus on relevant skills, learn in your preferred format, and build projects that demonstrate what you know.

## Get started

Send this repository link to Cubbie:

https://github.com/ssrid111/TECH-FOMO-Filter-

Approve Cubbie's installation prompt. Then send a new message:

> I want an ML internship at NVIDIA. I know Python, have four hours a week, and prefer videos.

Or send a voice note describing your goal. Cubbie asks one question at a time to fill in the details.

## From ambition to action

**Find the skills that matter.** Research relevant official job postings and separate required qualifications, preferred skills, and suggested preparation. Compare them with your current experience.

**Learn your way.** Choose videos, blogs/documentation, or a mix. Receive a focused resource and a small practice task instead of a long list of links.

**Choose a project worth finishing.** Compare three ranked projects based on role relevance, feasibility, demonstrable skills, and distinctiveness. Pick one and receive a step-by-step path.

**Make progress in small steps.** Get the current milestone and a clear definition of done. Share code, a screenshot, results, or an explanation for feedback.

**Talk it through.** Send voice notes for goals, blockers, project updates, and interview practice. Cubbie transcribes incoming audio and responds in short text.

**Check your progress weekly.** Take up to four short questions on what you practiced, answering by text or a short voice note. The quiz is capped at 10 minutes including feedback, with fewer questions when needed. Get an assessed-answer score, a strength, one correction, and a focused next action. The quiz starts when you say you are ready. Where automatic timing is unavailable, Cubbie asks you to use a phone timer; message delivery may be delayed.

**Stay consistent.** Opt into check-ins at a time and frequency you choose. Pause or change them whenever you need.

## Built for a text thread

- Short replies, normally 30–70 words.
- One question or next action at a time.
- At most three choices and two resources per reply.
- Full roadmaps available as documents.
- No long intake forms or curriculum dumps.

Project rankings use a transparent coaching rubric. Progress reflects evidence from practice and projects, rather than videos watched or a predicted hiring score. Requirements and resources are researched during use; employer examples are not claims about current openings.

## Requirements

An existing Cubbie account with its web, document, memory, and voice-transcription abilities. No additional API key, package installation, team backend, or Google/Canvas connection is needed. If an ability is unavailable, the Skill offers a student-provided or manual alternative.

This repository contains an instruction-based Cubbie Skill. Cubbie supplies the messaging and execution environment; there is no separate app to deploy. Incoming voice notes are supported through transcription; responses are text.

## Privacy and student control

Save learning preferences and progress only with permission. The Skill does not introduce a team server for resumes, audio, or progress. It instructs Cubbie to retain useful progress summaries rather than raw voice recordings or full transcripts; Cubbie's own service policies still apply.

Check-ins require consent. Applications, messages to others, publishing, purchases, and account changes require explicit confirmation. Tech FOMO Filter does not guarantee interviews or employment.
